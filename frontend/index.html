SentimentAI

Type a sentence. An embedding layer turns each word into a vector, a bidirectional LSTM reads the sequence, and a linear layer decides how it feels — negative, neutral, or positive.

  1. 1

    Clean & tokenize

    Lowercased, stripped of noise, split into word tokens and stemmed to their root form.

  2. 2

    Embed & encode

    Each token becomes a dense vector; a bidirectional LSTM tracks how the meaning unfolds.

  3. 3

    Classify

    A linear layer scores three classes and softmax turns the scores into a confidence.
